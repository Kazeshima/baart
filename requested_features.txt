To evaluate a student strength in arena battle in Blue Archive(pvp), 5 things should be considered:

* 盲打威力(blindshot power): this evaluate how strong the student is in a general attack without knowing the defense formation and students(in general only the 1st main student and the 2 supporter students are visible to attacker before attacking). A student that has a large covering angle of special skills with low cost is easier to have higher chance of doing damage to defense and win even without knowing the defense
* 进攻对策性(counter strategy or some better English name): this evaluate how strong the student is in a carefully chosen attack formation based on a given defense, which is called a "counter" to a known defense. The student can take advantage of the weapon range/positioning/targeting/armor-bullet damage multiplier to gain significant advantage and have very high win rate even if the skill cast is random. These students not necessarily is a general good option for blindshot
* 通防强度(general defense strength): this evaluate the defense win rate against some common attacks/blindshots. If it can tackle down many different formations and attacks and the defense success rate is higher the strength is considered as higher. This is like the defense version of 盲打威力(blindshot power)
* 特防对策性(counter-defense speciality or some better English name): a counter defense is like the counter attack to a given defense but it is the opposite, which means it counters a specific given attack. Some attack might equally 50/50 over many different defenses and is very difficult to build defense against. If a student is good at specialized counter-defense it can build a specific defense that can be 75% or even higher effective
* 造价(build cost): which evaluate how much resources needs to be invested into the student. e.g. If the student is farmable or free the cost is lower. If the student requires at least unique weapon level 50 or higher(UE50) the cost is really high and expensive. The less expensive the build cost is, the higher this rating should be
